How OOP Directly Enables Environmental Accounting

In traditional systems, environmental benefits are hardcoded or ignored. In EcoShare, Polymorphism allows each distinct resource type to calculate carbon offsets using its scientific domain logic:

  • • DonationItem: Quantifies avoided embodied carbon from new factory fabrication.
  • • RentalItem: Quantifies lifecycle extension and product-service system efficiency.
  • • RecyclableWaste: Quantifies landfill diversion through weight \(\times\) material circularity indices.
